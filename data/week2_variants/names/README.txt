Control rule:

Interpretation note:
These are name-based demographic proxy groups, not claims about any real person's identity. White/African-American are U.S. race-associated proxy categories, while Turkish/Indian are national-cultural name-associated proxy categories. Report them as separate name-group sensitivity conditions rather than collapsing them into one race variable.
